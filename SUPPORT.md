# Getting Help

- **Using Cortex:** read the docs at [cortex-md.tech/docs](https://cortex-md.tech/docs).
- **Questions and ideas:** open a
  [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/cortex-md/cortex-app/discussions) or use the *Question*
  issue template.
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/cortex-md/cortex-app/issues/new/choose) with
  steps to reproduce, your OS and app version.
- **Feature requests and roadmap:** check the [roadmap](https://cortex-md.tech/roadmap) first, then
  open a feature request.
- **Plugins and themes:** problems with a community extension go to its author or the
  [registry](https://github.com/cortex-md/registry/issues).
- **Security issues:** never in public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers triage issues as time allows. A clear reproduction is the fastest way to get help.
